Who is Ravi Kr. Ranjan?

Bihar Ka Sabse
Alag Educator

Ravi Kr. Ranjan — popularly known as R.K. Ranjan Sir — is one of Arrah's most recognized educators and academic mentors. Over the past 10+ years, he has built a reputation that is fundamentally different from conventional coaching teachers: he teaches students to think, not to memorize.

Holding an MA in English Literature, his academic depth goes far beyond what most coaching institutes offer. He has published 3 research papers at prestigious national institutions — a rare achievement in the world of coaching and regional education.

"In Bihar's education system, we have been training students to remember answers. Ravi Kr. Ranjan dared to ask: What if we trained them to understand instead? That question became RK Ranjan Classes."

Under his leadership, RK Ranjan Classes has become Arrah's #1 coaching institute — winning the Best Institute Award 2025, achieving 100% board results, producing district toppers, and earning coverage in both Dainik Jagran and Dainik Bhaskar.

Teaching Philosophy

The Principles Behind Every Class

Ravi Kr. Ranjan's teaching is not a method — it is a philosophy. One that was built over 10+ years of asking: How do students actually learn?

"My goal is not to complete the syllabus. My goal is to make sure not a single student leaves the class confused."

This single principle shapes everything at RK Ranjan Classes — from how concepts are introduced, to how doubts are handled, to how assessments are designed.

01
Concept Before Speed

Never rush a concept. If one student doesn't understand, the class doesn't move. Every student deserves the same clarity.

02
Zero Hesitation Culture

No question is too small. No doubt is shameful. Students who ask more, learn more. Creating this culture is his first priority in every batch.

03
Thinking Over Memorizing

Students are trained to reason through answers, not recall them. "Why" and "How" are valued far more than "What".

04
Regular Evaluation, No Surprises

His NEP-based 50-mark model ensures students are assessed every 2 weeks — building confidence steadily, eliminating exam shock.

05
Student as an Individual

With max 20 students per batch, he knows each student's strengths, weaknesses, and learning pace. No student is just a roll number.
