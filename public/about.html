Our story

Built to change how Bihar learns

RK Ranjan Classes was started by Ravi Kr. Ranjan with one bold goal: to show that students in Bihar can understand deeply, think clearly and score brilliantly when they are taught the right way.

It was never meant to be another coaching centre. It is a movement against rote learning, a place where every student is treated as a thinker and where concept clarity comes before speed.

  • NEP 2020 aligned from day one
  • Concept first teaching, no syllabus pressure
  • Maximum 20 students in a batch, so every child is known by name
  • Written test, oral, project and participation make up 50 marks
  • Continuous evaluation removes exam fear
  • Results covered by Dainik Jagran and Dainik Bhaskar

NEP 2020

Our 50 mark assessment model

Assessment is held every 2 weeks, on the 2nd and 4th Saturday of every month. Four pillars make sure a student grows in understanding, speech, creativity and character.

Written test30 marks, 60%
Project and activity10 marks, 20%
Oral and viva5 marks, 10%
Participation and homework5 marks, 10%
  • Written: 10 to 15 concept MCQs plus short and long answers
  • Oral: 1 or 2 questions, a 1 to 2 minute explanation
  • Project: model, diagram, survey or case study with a short explanation
  • Participation: attendance, homework, discipline and behaviour
