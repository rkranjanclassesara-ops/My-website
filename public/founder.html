Who is Ravi Kr. Ranjan?

Bihar ka sabse alag educator

Ravi Kr. Ranjan, popularly known as R.K. Ranjan Sir, is one of Arrah's most recognised educators and academic mentors. For over 10 years he has built a reputation that is different from usual coaching teachers: he teaches students to think, not to memorize.

With an MA in English Literature his academic depth goes far beyond what most coaching institutes offer. He has presented and published 3 research papers at respected institutions, which is rare in regional coaching.

We have been training students to remember answers. He dared to ask: what if we trained them to understand instead? That question became RK Ranjan Classes.

Teaching philosophy

The principles behind every class

A philosophy built over 10+ years of asking: how do students actually learn?

My goal is not to complete the syllabus. My goal is to make sure not a single student leaves the class confused.Ravi Kr. Ranjan
01

Concept before speed

Never rush a concept. If one student does not understand, the class does not move.

02

Zero hesitation culture

No question is too small, no doubt is shameful. Students who ask more, learn more.

03

Thinking over memorizing

Students reason through answers instead of recalling them. Why and how matter more than what.

04

Regular evaluation, no surprises

The 50 mark model assesses every 2 weeks, building confidence and removing exam shock.

05

Every student is an individual

With a maximum of 20 students per batch he knows every child's strengths and pace.

The journey

From teacher to founder

Step 1

Early career, 10+ years

He spends over a decade teaching and sees that students struggle not for lack of intelligence but because they are taught to memorize. His concept first approach takes shape.

Step 2

Research phase

He takes classroom observations to the academic world: presenting at Kristu Jayanti College Bengaluru and Govt. Girls PG College Ratlam, and publishing at VKSU Ara.

Step 3

The decision to build

After years of seeing bright students held back by a broken model, he decides to build an institute around concept clarity and NEP 2020.
