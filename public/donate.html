Constitutional right

Why education cannot be denied

Aao milkar is adhikar ko ground level par sach banayein.

Article 21

"No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law." Supreme Court ke mutabik Right to Life mein Right to Education bhi shamil hai.

Article 21-A

86th Amendment, 2002 se jodi gayi. 6 se 14 saal ke har bachche ko free aur compulsory education ka adhikar hai. Yeh favour nahi, fundamental right hai.
