⚖️ Constitutional Right

Article 21 & 21-A —
Why Education Cannot Be Denied

India ka Constitution desh ke har bacche ko education ka adhikar deta hai. Aao milke is adhikar ko ground level par sach banate hain.

📜 Article 21 — Constitution of India
"No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law."
Supreme Court ne kaha hai ki "Right to Life (Article 21) mein Right to Education bhi shamil hai." Ek bacche ko education se rokna — uski zindagi ki poorti se rokna hai.
📜 Article 21-A — Added by 86th Amendment, 2002
"The State shall provide free and compulsory education to all children of the age of six to fourteen years."
Source: Constitution of India · Right to Education Act, 2009
क्या कहता है Constitution? What does it say?

Article 21-A guarantees every child between 6-14 years the right to free and compulsory education. This is a Fundamental Right — not a favour, not charity.
